Accomplishments → Social Media Identifier

Scans the monthly Accomplishments page, flags the items worth posting on the University LinkedIn, and drafts a short, ready-to-post version of each — based on the principle: we don't want activity, we want distinction.

1Paste the Accomplishments page link

Paste any link to the eConnection Accomplishments page (or a specific month), then click Identify. The app fetches the page and analyzes it automatically.

View the selection criteria this app applies
  • A. Honors & Recognition: elected Fellow of a professional society; national/international awards (excludes internal S&T awards and "best paper" awards).
  • B. Leadership Roles: keynote speeches; plenary talks; chairing a major conference or serving on a Board of Directors; President / Vice-President / Chair / Co-chair roles; Editor-in-Chief / editorial board lead.
  • C. Competitive Achievements: winning or placing in regional / national / international competitions (especially students).
  • D. Research & Funding Milestones: large grants over $1M (especially federal — NSF, NIH, DoD); breakthrough outcomes (Science, Nature, PNAS).
  • E. Institutional / External Impact: research featured in major media; work influencing policy / industry standards; patents licensed or commercialization milestones.
  • Excluded as "activity, not distinction": presented a paper, attended a conference, generic talks, routine participation.

This tool applies keyword-and-pattern rules to the criteria document and is meant as a first-pass filter; a human should confirm each pick before posting.
Per the criteria, every selected item also needs a photo or video before going live on LinkedIn.
